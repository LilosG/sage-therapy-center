Carlsbad clients — whether in La Costa, Olde Carlsbad, Bressi Ranch, Aviara, or Calavera Hills — come to EMDR sessions from different starting points: some specifically seeking it out by name, others introduced to it partway through individual or couples work once it becomes clear it may help with what they're working through.

EMDR is offered alongside a broader range of approaches — Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work — so a Carlsbad client's sessions aren't limited to a single method if something else fits better as the work develops. Because the office is local, ongoing EMDR work can fit into a regular Carlsbad routine without a long drive attached to every appointment.

EMDR is closely related to individual therapy, also offered at the Carlsbad office, since EMDR sessions are typically one-on-one. If you're unsure whether EMDR specifically is the right starting point versus individual therapy more broadly, that's a good question to bring to a first conversation rather than something to decide on your own beforehand.

Clients tend to arrive at EMDR from one of two directions: either they've read about it or heard about it specifically and want to know if it's a fit, or they're already in individual or couples work and EMDR comes up partway through as something that might help with what they're processing. Neither path is more "correct" than the other, and neither locks you in — because EMDR is one approach among several Kristin offers, it's possible to start with it and shift to something else, or the reverse, as the work continues.
