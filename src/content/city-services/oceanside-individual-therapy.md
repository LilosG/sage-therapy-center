Oceanside residents looking for individual therapy work with S.A.G.E. Therapy Center at the Carlsbad office — 5055 Avenida Encinas, Suite 100 — just south of Oceanside. There's no separate Oceanside location; every individual therapy session takes place at the Carlsbad address.

Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT, works one on one with adults, drawing on EMDR, Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based approaches, chosen based on what fits your situation rather than a single fixed method.

People generally come to individual therapy for one of a few reasons: feeling overwhelmed or stuck, anxiety, depression, anger, or ongoing stress, patterns of low self-worth or self-sabotage, relationship or boundary difficulties, trauma, abuse, or PTSD, or concerns like sleep problems, addiction, or disordered eating. If any of this describes what you're going through, that's a reasonable starting point for reaching out — and if you're in crisis right now, please contact 911 or a crisis line rather than waiting for a scheduled session.

Because there's no Oceanside office, sessions are in person at the Carlsbad address, not remote and not at a location closer to Oceanside. That's worth weighing honestly against the range of approaches available at the Carlsbad office.

Individual therapy is one of two services currently available to Oceanside clients, alongside couples counseling — both held at the same Carlsbad office.
