S.A.G.E. Therapy Center is based in Carlsbad, and San Marcos residents are welcome at the Avenida Encinas office — a short drive inland. Individual therapy and couples counseling are both offered there, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT, drawing on a range of approaches including EMDR, Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work depending on what fits.

There is no separate San Marcos office. Every session, whether you're coming from San Marcos or elsewhere in North County San Diego, takes place at the Carlsbad location.

## San Marcos FAQs

**Do you have an office in San Marcos?** No. S.A.G.E. Therapy Center's only office is in Carlsbad, at 5055 Avenida Encinas, Suite 100 — a short drive from San Marcos.

**What can San Marcos clients expect?** The same individual therapy and couples counseling offered to Carlsbad clients, held at the Carlsbad office.
