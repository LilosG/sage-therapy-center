San Marcos is an inland North County city with a genuinely different pace than the coastal cities S.A.G.E. Therapy Center also serves — it's not a beach town. It's home to California State University San Marcos, with around 17,000 students, and known for being family-oriented, with residential communities like San Elijo Hills. S.A.G.E. Therapy Center is based in Carlsbad, and San Marcos residents are welcome at the Avenida Encinas office — a short drive from San Marcos out to the coast. Individual therapy and couples counseling are both offered there, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT, drawing on a range of approaches including EMDR, Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work depending on what fits.

There is no separate San Marcos office. Every session, whether you're coming from San Marcos or elsewhere in North County San Diego, takes place at the Carlsbad location.
