S.A.G.E. Therapy Center is based in Carlsbad, just south of Oceanside, and Oceanside residents are welcome at the Avenida Encinas office. Individual therapy and couples counseling are both offered there, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT, drawing on a range of approaches including EMDR, Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work depending on what fits.

There is no separate Oceanside office. Every session, whether you're coming from Oceanside or elsewhere in North County San Diego, takes place at the Carlsbad location.
