S.A.G.E. Therapy Center is based in Carlsbad, and Encinitas residents are welcome at the Avenida Encinas office — just a short drive up the coast. Individual therapy and couples counseling are both offered there, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT, drawing on a range of approaches including EMDR, Lifespan Integration, CBT, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work depending on what fits.

Encinitas clients, including those from Olivenhain, are a regular part of the practice. There is no separate Encinitas office — every session, whether you're coming from Encinitas or elsewhere in North County, takes place at the Carlsbad location.
