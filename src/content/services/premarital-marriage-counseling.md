Premarital and marriage counseling at S.A.G.E. Therapy Center offers support for couples preparing for marriage or working through the specific stage of a committed partnership. Sessions take place at the Carlsbad office, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT.

An approach can include IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work, chosen based on what fits the relationship. Some couples seek premarital counseling to build a strong foundation before marriage; others come to marriage counseling navigating a stage-specific challenge together.

Request a session to discuss fit and availability.
