Teen counseling at S.A.G.E. Therapy Center offers support for teens navigating anxiety, life transitions, or family conflict. Sessions take place at the Carlsbad office, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT.

An approach can include CBT, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work, chosen based on what fits your teen. Some families seek teen counseling when a teen is feeling anxious or overwhelmed, going through a difficult transition, or navigating conflict at home.

Request a session to discuss fit and availability.
