## Couples come to therapy for many different reasons

Couples counseling at S.A.G.E. Therapy Center offers support for partners working through communication, connection, or relationship challenges. Sessions take place at the Carlsbad office with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT.

Some couples reach out because they feel stuck in a recurring cycle. Others are navigating a difficult transition, trying to reconnect, or simply want dedicated space to understand how they communicate and relate to one another.

### Sessions may explore

- recurring communication patterns
- conflict and repair
- emotional connection and distance
- trust and relationship strain
- transitions affecting the relationship
- patterns that feel difficult to change together

## An approach shaped around the relationship

Kristin does not use one fixed method for every couple. An approach can include IMAGO, attachment-based, psychodynamic, experiential, CBT, or EMDR-informed work depending on what is relevant to the relationship and within the scope of the work.

The goal of the first conversation is not to arrive with everything figured out. It is to begin clarifying what is happening, what each partner is experiencing, and what you would like to understand through therapy.

> Requesting a session is a chance to discuss fit and availability before deciding what the next step should be.
