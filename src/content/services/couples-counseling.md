Couples counseling at S.A.G.E. Therapy Center offers support for partners working through communication, connection, or relationship challenges. Sessions take place at the Carlsbad office, with Kristin Moorehead-Malley, LMFT.

An approach can include EMDR, IMAGO, psychodynamic, and attachment-based work, chosen based on what fits the relationship. Some couples seek counseling when they're feeling stuck in recurring patterns, navigating a difficult transition together, or simply want to strengthen how they communicate and connect.

Request a session to discuss fit and availability.
